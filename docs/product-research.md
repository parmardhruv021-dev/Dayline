# Jibble product research

**Date:** 2026-10-27  
**Scope:** Public official Jibble marketing/product pages and official Help Center articles.  
**Research method:** Page content was fetched and summarized using web research. No logged-in product UI, private API, or user-owned browser session was inspected. “Verified” below means *stated in an official source*, not independently exercised.

## Product structure reported by public sources

Jibble organizes its publicly described capabilities around time tracking, attendance verification, timesheets/payroll, team management, and projects/reporting. Public pages also advertise time off, approvals, schedules, integrations, kiosk and offline operation. The marketing site does not provide a reliable complete navigation map of the signed-in product.

**Sources:** [Jibble homepage](https://www.jibble.io/), [features](https://www.jibble.io/features), [time-and-attendance software](https://www.jibble.io/time-and-attendance-software).

## What was not established

- Signed-in navigation, actual screen layouts, exact validation/error states, loading/empty states, or interaction persistence.
- Password reset/session expiry behavior, onboarding steps, invitation acceptance details, and tenant-specific role configuration.
- Exact rounding, overtime, break-pay, DST, midnight, late/grace, missing-punch and concurrent event algorithms for every configuration.
- Current subscription-tier entitlements, comprehensive retention/backup policy, and complete integrations list.
- A comprehensive administrative audit log beyond documented time-entry history.
