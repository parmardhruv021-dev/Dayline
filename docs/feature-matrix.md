## Priority reasoning

P0 prioritizes trustworthy attendance, access isolation, correction/approval accountability, usable timesheets, and real exports. The present product is a limited, tested milestone rather than the complete P0 scope; see [architecture](architecture.md) and [implementation plan](implementation-plan.md) for boundaries. Leave and schedule administration are P1 until office rules are available; optional surveillance-style verification, offline queueing, and provider integrations are P2 because their privacy/operational complexity is not justified for the first release. Any employment/payroll calculation remains subject to explicit office policy and jurisdiction review.
